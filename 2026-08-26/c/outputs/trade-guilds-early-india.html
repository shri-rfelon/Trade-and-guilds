Case study

THE NĀŚHIK CAVE INSCRIPTION

Monetary endowments could be deposited with guilds, and interest could support religious establishments. This shows why some guilds were trusted as financial institutions and trustees, not only as professional organisations.

“Some guilds were trusted not only as professional organisations, but also as financial institutions and trustees.”

ARCHAEOLOGICAL EVIDENCE

Roman coins, amphora fragments and Mediterranean pottery provide material evidence for long-distance trade connections. Maritime trade connected inland production centres with ports including Muziris, Kāveripaṭṭinam and Arikameḍu.

08 · Conclusion

TRADE CONNECTED INDIA.GUILDS CREATED COLLECTIVE ECONOMIC POWER.

🧵 Workshop→🏛 Guild→🏪 Market→⚓ Port→🌍 World

Trade in early India was much more than the exchange of goods. It connected regions, cities, ports and overseas networks. Guilds organised artisans and merchants, regulated professional activities, managed wealth and could even act as financial institutions.

“Trade connected India, while guilds transformed individual skills into collective economic power.”
